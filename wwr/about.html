Exciting Expeditions

At Exciting Expeditions, our white water rafting trips are designed to provide thrill-seekers with an unforgettable experience on the river. Our certified guides prioritize safety while you navigate stunning landscapes and exciting rapids. Oars Up!

History

Exciting Expeditions was founded in 1995 by a group of river enthusiasts who wanted to share their passion for outdoor adventure. Starting with just a few rafts and local river routes, the company has grown into a trusted leader in guided white water rafting expeditions.
